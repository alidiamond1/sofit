# SoFit Marketing Design System

## Direction

**Editorial athlete studio** — disciplined, premium, direct. The site should feel like one experienced coach with a precise method, not a crowded commercial gym or a generic SaaS template.

## Visual language

- Canvas: `#050605`
- Raised surface: `#101310`
- Quiet surface: `#171A17`
- Primary text: `#F4F7F1`
- Secondary text: `#A6AFA5`
- Brand accent: `#3AD366`
- Accent ink: `#041006`
- Hairline: `rgba(255,255,255,.10)`
- Focus ring: `#8AF3A7`
- Corners: 16px for controls, 24–32px for editorial media, 999px only for compact CTAs/tags
- Shadow: deep and soft; use borders for most hierarchy and reserve shadows for floating navigation/media

## Typography

- Display: condensed, uppercase, high-impact treatment using the project-safe display fallback stack
- Body: the optimized project sans, 16px minimum on mobile, 1.55–1.75 line height
- Small labels: uppercase, 11–12px, 0.12–0.18em tracking
- Desktop paragraph measure: 60–72 characters

## Layout

- Mobile-first gutters: 20px; tablet: 32px; desktop: 48px
- Maximum content width: 1240px
- Section rhythm: 72px mobile / 112px desktop
- Prefer asymmetric 12-column editorial compositions over equal repeated cards
- Home is the richest page: split hero, proof rail, program matrix, method steps, coach story, portal preview, FAQ, closing CTA
- Internal pages have a concise editorial hero followed by deep, scannable content

## Interaction

- Primary target size: at least 44×44px
- Motion: 180–280ms; opacity/transform only; no decorative looping
- One primary CTA per section
- Keep visible keyboard focus and active navigation state
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Photography

- The real coach image (`/brand/hero.jpg`) is the brand anchor and must remain recognizable
- Supporting AI imagery must avoid introducing a competing trainer identity
- Use monochrome/low-saturation treatment with natural skin tones restored on hover only where useful
- Always reserve image dimensions and use meaningful alt text

## Content rules

- Do not invent client counts, transformation numbers, certifications, testimonials, or prices
- Emphasize the real service model: consultation, diet plan, workout plan, and personal training tiers
- Voice: concise, assured, practical, never shouty or motivational-cliché heavy

## Avoid

- Purple gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, emoji icons, neon cyberpunk effects
- Pill-shaped containers for ordinary cards or text
- Dense dashboards on marketing pages
- Generic “transform your life” claims without evidence
- Copying 21st.dev components verbatim or importing patterns unrelated to fitness coaching
